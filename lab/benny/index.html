Benny the Bird.

  • Game
  • JavaScript
  • p5.js
  • Pixel art
  • 2024

Benny is a tree swallow who tried to fly and failed. Now he has to bounce his way back up to the nest, leaf by leaf, dodging a squirrel that guards the tree. A Doodle Jump-style game built from scratch in JavaScript, with hand-made pixel art, sound effects and music.

Bounce on the leaves, climb as high as you can and watch out for the battle squirrel. Your high score is saved in your browser. Made in a two-person team for Foundations of Programming at Jönköping University, with p5.js, pixel art, sound effects and music.
