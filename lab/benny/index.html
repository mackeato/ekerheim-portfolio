Benny the Bird.

  • Game
  • Vanilla JavaScript
  • Canvas
  • Pixel art
  • 2024

Benny is a tree swallow who tried to fly and failed. Now he has to bounce his way back up to the nest, leaf by leaf. A Doodle Jump-style game built from scratch in JavaScript, with hand-made pixel art.
