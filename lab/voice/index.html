Voice mirror.

  • Creative coding
  • Web Audio API
  • Canvas 2D
  • 2025

An interactive piece about human expression and machine perception. The camera shows you, and your voice decides how much of you survives: low hums make the image wobble, loud high sounds tear it into glitch bands.

Needs your camera and microphone. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Try humming low, then say a long “sssss”. Best in a quiet room.

ListenAn AnalyserNode measures loudness (RMS) and the spectral centroid as a pitch estimate.
SeeEvery frame is split into RGB channels, posterized and traced with a Sobel edge filter.
ReactVolume drives a sine wobble per row; high pitch plus volume switches to glitch bands.
