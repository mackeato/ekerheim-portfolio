00 Now — 2026

Relaunching Luftix.

In 2026 I stepped in as CEO and co-owner of Luftix Nordic AB, a Swedish retailer of air-to-air heat pumps with certified installation nationwide. The company had been dormant, so we restarted it.

It's where everything I've learned comes together. I run and keep developing the Shopify store, set up the paid search, and handle the business side: suppliers, installation partners and weekly KPI reports to the board. Right now I'm building a way for customers to get an accurate installation price before they check out.

My ground rules

Privacy first
Customer data and anything confidential stays out of prompts. I follow GDPR and each tool's data policy.
Trust, but verify
AI can be confidently wrong. I check claims against sources and never ship code I can't explain.
Be open about it
I'm transparent about where AI played a part, this site included.
Keep the craft human
AI speeds up the work; it doesn't replace the idea. The creative decisions are mine.

In practice
